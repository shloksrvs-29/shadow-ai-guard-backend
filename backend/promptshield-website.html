Generative AI moved into every workflow almost overnight — and so did the risk of employees pasting things they shouldn't.

Shadow AI — the use of AI tools outside a company's visibility or approval — is invisible to normal security tools, because it travels over ordinary encrypted web traffic. PromptShield doesn't try to ban AI use. It sits at the one place that actually matters: the moment before a message is sent, checking it locally and blocking anything sensitive before it reaches the AI provider.

What it catches

Real-time interception

Watches typing, pasting, and submission on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude — checked before anything is sent, not after.

Hybrid detection engine

A regex engine catches structured secrets (API keys, database credentials, private keys) instantly, running alongside a fine-tuned transformer model for unstructured PII like names, emails, and addresses.

Tamper-evident audit log

Every blocked and flagged submission is recorded in a hash-chained log, so security teams can verify nothing was altered after the fact.

India-specific identifiers

Detects Aadhaar numbers (validated with the Verhoeff checksum, not just pattern-matching) and PAN cards, alongside standard global PII types.

Self-hosted by design

The backend runs on your own machine or infrastructure. Sensitive prompts are never sent to a third party for analysis.

How it works

1

Watching the input

A content script watches the message box, paste events, and the send action on supported AI chat sites.

2

Checking before sending

Before the message is submitted, the extension sends the text to your PromptShield backend for analysis.

3

Two detectors, run together

A regex engine flags structured secrets; a fine-tuned DistilBERT model flags unstructured PII — both run in parallel.

4

One decision, explained

Results are combined into a single allow-or-block decision, with a plain explanation of what was found.

5

Blocked, not silently dropped

If something sensitive is found, nothing is sent — and the employee sees exactly why, right where they were typing.
